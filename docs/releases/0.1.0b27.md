# 0.1.0b27 — Per-room maximum duty cycle

A radiator that cools quickly can reach its restart temperature soon after each pulse. With a persistent room deficit, ordinary cycles then approach 50% open time. This release adds an optional per-room limit.

**Maximum duty cycle** (default 100%, no limit) keeps the valve closed after an ordinary or calibration pulse for at least `open time × (100 − limit) / limit`: twice the open time at 33%, three times at 25%. It is an additional reopening condition; room demand and the temperature-based cooling gate are unchanged and still required. Window PWM and timeout recovery keep their own timing.

Each room gets a **Maximum duty cycle** select entity (No limit, 50%, 33%, 25%). A selection applies immediately, including to a pause in progress, and is stored in the entry options without reloading the entry or discarding learned state. Any value from 1 to 100 can also be entered in the options form. Changing the limit does not invalidate a saved calibration or restart gate.

While the duty pause alone prevents reopening, Phase shows `cooling` and Last decision shows `duty_limit`; Phase attributes include `max_duty_percent` and `duty_resume_at`. A pending duty pause is not restored after a restart.

Restart Home Assistant after updating. The limit caps heat output: a room with a high target may settle lower or rely more on other heat sources.
