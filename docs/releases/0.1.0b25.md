# 0.1.0b25 — Verify valve limits and validate calibration candidates

A late opening-limit reset could leave a pulse reporting heating while the device held an incompatible pair of limits. MQTT valves now require a transaction-correlated Zigbee read of both Sonoff attributes after opening, closing and firmware frost handoff. Cached state alone cannot confirm delivery. An owned open pulse also checks once a minute and immediately on a conflicting entity update. A confirmed drift triggers a safe close/reopen under the existing demand and original maximum-duration deadline; its earlier thermal trace is discarded. Failed confirmation enters the existing ten minute-spaced reconnection probes before a room-only emergency.

The read-only request uses Zigbee2MQTT's `bridge/request/action` raw ZCL read interface, verified against Zigbee2MQTT 2.14.2. Device identity, current network address and endpoint come from MQTT discovery and bridge metadata. Retained, unrelated, incomplete and incorrectly addressed replies cannot confirm a command. This verifies device configuration attributes, not mechanical stem position or water flow. Released firmware frost protection remains autonomous.

A newly detected plateau is now accepted only after its lag-compensated closing threshold has been validated. An invalid falling-tail candidate preserves the previous usable reference, threshold and measurement age, closes the pulse, and requests recalibration on the next eligible pulse. Without a usable previous reference, the existing bounded thermal-recovery cycle remains in effect.

Existing per-entry tuning is preserved. Window PWM, thermostat requests, frost thresholds, the logical 0/5 graph and notification/reserve policy are unchanged.

Restart Home Assistant after installing. The Phase sensor includes the last valve-pair readback, verification time and correction count for diagnosis.
