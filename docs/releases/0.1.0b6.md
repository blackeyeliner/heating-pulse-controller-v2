Open-window cooling could prevent a radiator from reaching its saved cutoff or forming a plateau, leaving a pulse open until the maximum-duration fault. Beta 6 adds an explicit window mode.

- Optional window cover selection through Configure. Open-window heating starts with an eight-minute pause, then uses up to two minutes at the existing calibrated minimum and at least eight minutes closed. Room satisfaction skips/truncates pulses. Window-exposed measurements never update calibration or lag.
- With Control enabled on, room ≤16 °C or radiator ≤10 °C immediately requests emergency window closure. No preliminary heating experiment. Works during heating faults; retries are limited to once per 30 seconds. It never opens windows and cannot prove physical closure.
- New Window status diagnostic and Window opening allowed interlock for external opening automations. Unknown cover state inhibits heating. Closed windows return to ordinary control with preserved valid calibration.
- Existing rooms without a selected window retain their algorithm and entities. Restart/reload remains passive and requires fresh reports plus explicit enable. Faults are not silently reset by window events.

Update through HACS, restart HA, select the window in Configure, and add the interlock to the relevant opening automations before enabling control. Heating command timing is not a hard-real-time or frost-protection guarantee.

Validation includes deterministic PWM timing, delayed callbacks, exact temperature boundaries, interrupted calibration and lag learning, unavailable covers, disabled/off states, emergency closure with a heating fault, service failures, in-flight valve inhibition, real HA entities/options removal and the previous regression suite.
