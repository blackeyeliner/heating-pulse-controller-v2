A calibration timeout previously closed the valve and latched all subsequent heating off. Beta 11 keeps bounded maintenance heat and automatically retries calibration.

- Increase the ordinary/calibration opening limit from 15 to 20 minutes. At timeout, close for at least 80 minutes (longer if closure was late), then recalibrate on room demand. The timed-out opening already counts as the on portion; no additional PWM pulse is inserted.
- Data/calibration failures use non-latching recovery PWM. Closed window: up to 20 minutes on / at least 80 minutes off. Open or unknown window: up to 15 minutes on / at least 45 minutes off, per the pilot owner’s corrected 25% window policy.
- Preserve a running pulse’s original start. Suspend plateau/lag learning during recovery. Fresh room satisfaction skips/truncates heating; missing room measurements allow bounded maintenance heat while heat mode is requested. Explicit off remains authoritative. A missing room thermostat uses the last valid request/setpoint, never a guessed initial target.
- After the timed pause, valid inputs permit a calibration retry; otherwise PWM repeats. Persist the pause and retry intent through updates/reloads without resuming an open pulse or enabling automatically. Increasing only the old 900-second limit to 1200 seconds preserves compatible calibration and restart thresholds.
- Only failed valve commands/unavailable physical valve entities latch actuator_error. A failed graph-helper write is logged without failing valve control. If emergency window closure fails, continue maintenance PWM while retrying the window command. Legacy-writer activation remains an ownership handover: yield closed and resume eligibility when those writers are off.

Validation covers long calibrations, repeated timed recovery with missing sensors, automatic calibration retry, room satisfaction/off, window changes, pause persistence, actual HA actuator deadlines and thermostat/valve outages. These are software/command tests; physical flow, communication outages and room frost protection are not guaranteed by them.
