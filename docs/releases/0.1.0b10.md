Restart temperature now persists independently of confirmed cooling episodes. Earlier releases saved only an episode with a measured peak, losing a startup/current threshold during repeated updates or window-affected operation.

- Persist the last selected threshold and its source (startup, base, adaptive, manual), with matching algorithm settings and device bindings. Keep it through periods without a new peak; do not manufacture peak/lag observations.
- Restore the threshold after fresh sensor reports, known window feedback and closed TRV limits. Current demand and cooling readiness are checked again; enabled state, open pulses and PWM timers are never resumed. The parameter can survive longer downtimes; current sensor freshness remains mandatory.
- Retain beta 7 full episode restoration when eligible, with threshold-only fallback otherwise. Restart temperature exposes source and restored_gate status.
- New set_restart_temperature action accepts config_entry_id and temperature for a one-time current gate adjustment while not heating and without a fault. It is persisted but is replaced by the next normally calculated gate.

Validation includes startup/calculated/manual gates without peaks, long downtime, changed/corrupt bindings/settings, unknown feedback, passive setup, repeated real HA storage reloads and the real service action. Window policy and room setpoint are unchanged.
