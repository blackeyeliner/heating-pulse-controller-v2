Open-window PWM now uses up to **10 minutes at the calibrated minimum opening, followed by at least 40 minutes closed**. The nominal maximum duty remains 20%; the longer pulse accommodates slower radiator warm-up. The initial closed interval is also 40 minutes.

Room satisfaction can still skip or truncate a pulse. Cold-threshold window closure, stale-data inhibition, maximum-open limits, no learning during window exposure, and the delayed-closure extension of the next pause remain in effect. Closed-window adaptive heating and beta 7 cooling persistence are unchanged. Reload starts disabled and begins a new window pause after explicit enable; no immediate heating pulse is forced by the update.

Validation covers three full 50-minute PWM periods, remaining open beyond the old two-minute cutoff, closure at ten minutes, the forty-minute pause, delayed callbacks, and existing window/cooling lifecycle cases.
