# Beta17: recognise gently declining radiator plateaus

Minute-spaced reports previously needed three consecutive eligible samples, effectively doubling the configured one-minute confirmation. Mild cooling could then discard the candidate and keep an otherwise hot radiator open until its maximum-duration timeout.

Confirmation now uses elapsed time between fresh eligible reports, tolerates bounded gentle cooling after credible warming, and checks the confirmation segment as well as the rolling regression. Renewed warming hidden by a lagging regression is rejected. Cold flat starts, rapid falls, missing reports and report gaps remain ineligible.

Regression coverage includes recorded plateau traces, calibration and ordinary lower-reference replacement, and 10/30/60-second reporting. Replay checks detector behaviour on the original trace; it does not predict the physical response to different valve commands.

No room tuning, maximum-open limits, cooling gates, window PWM, emergency handling, native thermostat UI or enabled-state persistence changed. Heating-budget controls are not included in this release.
